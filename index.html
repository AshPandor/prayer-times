    Settings

    Masjid Zeenatul Islam on Stoney Stanton Road publishes its own beginning and jamaat times. Calculated times are for when you are away from the mosque.

    Moonsighting Committee is a solid default in the UK. Many mosques print Muslim World League times. Pick the one your mosque uses. Around midsummer, far northern latitudes make Fajr and Isha unusual; Moonsighting Committee is built for that.

    Hanafi Asr is later in the afternoon. Standard is the earlier Asr used by the Shafi, Maliki and Hanbali schools. The default here is Hanafi, which is common in the UK.

    On the mosque timetable this fires before each beginning time and before jamaat, when jamaat is later. Reminders only run while this page is open. A true background alert — one that still arrives hours after you have closed the app — needs a server, and this version does not have one. On an iPhone, website notifications only work after you add the app to the Home Screen, and only on iOS 16.4 or later.
